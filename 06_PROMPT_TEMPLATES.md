# 06 — Plantillas de Prompt

Estas plantillas no deben copiarse mecánicamente. Se adaptan a la historia.

## A. Plantilla — Imagen de referencia

```text
Vertical 9:16 authentic amateur smartphone still.

Biblical setting: [LUGAR / ÉPOCA].
Main event: [MOMENTO].
Camera is [DISTANCIA] from the action, recorded by a hidden witness from [POSICIÓN FÍSICA CREÍBLE].

Subjects remain small/medium in frame. The composition is slightly imperfect and observational, not heroic.

Foreground obstruction: [VEGETACIÓN / GENTE / MURO / SOMBRA / NINGUNO].
Do not use the same hiding element repeatedly.

Real cellphone look:
slightly soft detail,
visible digital noise,
mild compression,
limited distant detail,
imperfect autofocus,
slight exposure irregularity,
no cinematic color grading,
no ultra sharp detail,
no polished movie lighting.

Biblical accuracy:
[HECHOS QUE DEBEN VERSE].
Do not show:
[HECHOS NO DESCRITOS / ERRORES A EVITAR].
```

## B. Plantilla — Video 1 (15 s)

```text
Use the reference image as the exact starting frame.

15-second vertical 9:16 authentic amateur cellphone recording.

This is the first fragment of the same biblical story: [HISTORIA / PASAJE].

The hidden witness records from [POSICIÓN]. Keep the same physical camera location and believable distance.

Main action only:
[UNA ACCIÓN PRINCIPAL].

Biblical continuity:
[QUÉ DEBE PASAR].
[QUÉ NO DEBE INVENTARSE].

Physics:
keep body orientation consistent,
no teleporting,
no sudden 180-degree turns,
no disappearing,
no changing scale,
no impossible movement,
objects obey gravity and collision.

Camera:
tiny irregular hand tremor,
slight pulse,
small framing drift,
occasional autofocus hesitation,
optional brief imperfect digital zoom if useful,
focus may drift and recover naturally,
no cinematic movement,
no crane,
no dolly,
no smooth pan,
no floating camera.

Image quality:
soft smartphone detail,
visible noise,
compression,
imperfect focus,
reduced distant detail,
natural handheld motion blur,
not sharp,
not polished.

Audio:
[EFECTOS], plus subtle hidden-recording sounds if the witness is concealed (branches, leaves, cloth rustle, contained breathing).
Dialogue only if biblically appropriate, in natural Latin American Spanish:
[DIÁLOGO]. If Jesus speaks, his voice should feel warm, calm, firm, compassionate and authoritative, never generic or theatrical.

No music.
No narration unless explicitly requested.

The clip ends naturally while the event is still ongoing or at a believable transition into Video 2.
```

## C. Plantilla — Video 2 (15 s)

```text
Use the approved Video 2 reference image as the exact starting frame.

This is the continuation of the same biblical story from Video 1.

Preserve:
same people,
same clothing,
same location,
same physical state,
same time of day,
same environment,
same object positions unless they changed naturally in Video 1.

Main action only:
[UNA ACCIÓN PRINCIPAL DEL VIDEO 2].

Do not recap Video 1.
Do not reset the miracle.
Do not undo previous physical changes.

Biblical accuracy:
[SECUENCIA REAL].

Physics and camera rules:
[same permanent rules].

Dialogue, if present:
natural Latin American Spanish,
short,
emotionally believable,
consistent with the biblical text.

Audio:
[EFECTOS], plus subtle hidden-recording sounds if applicable.

If Jesus speaks, his voice should feel warm, calm, firm, compassionate and authoritative, in natural Latin American Spanish.

No music unless explicitly requested.
```
